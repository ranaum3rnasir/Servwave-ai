Phase 0 — MVP
Lead (manual entry) → Estimate(s) → 1 Approved → Deposit Paid → Office clicks “Create Job” → Job → Complete → Invoice → Payment

Leads (manual entry):

Manual lead creation (pipeline basics)

You didn’t list “lead” as its own section, so treat it as a lightweight “pre-customer record” that later becomes a customer + estimate. (This is an MVP design decision, not in the catalog.)

Estimates:

Create estimates (mobile + office), quick UX, line items, totals

Notes + photo attachments (strong field need)

Send by email/SMS link, one-tap approval, approval notifications

Multiple estimates per lead (MVP rule: only one can be “Approved” at a time)

Deposit (Mode A gating):

Deposit upfront amount on estimate (request + record payment)

Hard rule: deposit paid → office manually clicks Create Job (your choice)

Job management:

Create job (from estimate), job notes, customer assignment, date/time, attachments

Core statuses: Pending/Assigned/In Progress/Completed (+ your internal “scheduled” state)

Scheduling:

Week view calendar + unassigned job pool + manual assignment

Mobile tech workflow

Today’s jobs, job details, status updates, notes, photos

Invoicing:

Invoice from completed job + pro formatting + numbering + PDF

Payments:

Online payment link in invoice (card payments at minimum)

Payment status tracking (sent/viewed/paid, overdue optional)

Basic Reports & Analytics

0.2 Customer basics (just enough to operate)

Customer profiles + multiple locations/contacts + service history internal view

Search/filtering + duplicate prevention (phone/email)

0.3 “Help-through” features (MVP-minimum, but real)

These aren’t “nice-to-have” — they prevent your own team from hating the tool.

In-app help entry point (link to docs), basic FAQs / quick start guides (lightweight)

A tiny onboarding checklist (company setup, add techs, create first lead/estimate)


Explicitly NOT in MVP:

Data import/export, migration tooling (push to Phase 1.5)

Phase 1.5 — “Go-to-market readiness” (needed before onboarding external SMBs at scale)
1.5.1 Switching kit (migration + data hygiene)

CSV import (customers + open jobs/invoices at minimum)

CSV export (customers, jobs, invoices, payments)

Dedupe + validation (basic)

1.5.2 Support & onboarding foundation

Knowledge base, short video tutorials, onboarding wizard (lean version)

Phase 2 — Retention add-ons (make the product feel “complete” to customers)
2.1 Customer portal + self-serve (high retention)

Customer portal, invoice viewing, estimate approval, online payments, receipts

2.2 Communication add-ons (reduce office workload)

Two-way SMS + templates + message status tracking

Email templates + tracking/logging

2.3 Reminders

Customer reminders (email/SMS) + customizable timing

Phase 3 — “Estimating is the weapon” (your differentiation layer)
3.1 Quote sophistication

Good/Better/Best options, comparison view, upsell suggestions

3.2 Pricebook (start simple, then deepen)

Service list management + quick-select items

Then later: tiers by customer type, seasonal pricing

3.3 Estimate analytics (sales performance)

Conversion rate, estimate aging, by tech/service type (later in Phase 3)

Phase 4 — Operations scale (when you have real volume and complexity)
4.1 Advanced dispatch + optimization

Conflict detection, skill matching suggestions, auto-assignment rules

Route optimization + map view + travel time

4.2 Workforce management

Availability, shifts, vacation/time off, capacity limits, GPS tracking

Report builder, scheduled delivery, drilldowns, forecasting

Phase 5 — Platform + Integrations ecosystem (unlock partnerships + mid-market)

QuickBooks online sync (invoices + payments) and later two-way sync

5.2 Public API + webhooks

Public REST API, webhooks, OAuth, rate limiting

“CRM Co-Pilot” for office commands + data lookup (create jobs/estimates faster)
