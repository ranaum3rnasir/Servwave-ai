# Phase 0 — MVP (Replacement-ready core + basic reporting)

**MVP goal:** Your team can run the full workflow inside the new system while dual-running (old CRM in parallel), with manual lead entry, one-way comms, deposit required before job creation, and office manually clicks “Create Job.”

## Operating workflow (Phase 0)

**Lead (manual entry) → Estimate(s) → 1 Approved → Deposit Paid → Office clicks “Create Job” → Job → Complete → Invoice → Payment**

---

## 0.1 Users & Roles

### Roles (minimum)
- **Owner/Admin:** full access + settings  
- **Office Manager:** leads, estimates, deposits, jobs, schedule, invoices, payments, reports  
- **Technician:** assigned jobs only + customer contact info for those jobs  

### Must-have controls
- Invite/deactivate users  
- Basic permission enforcement (no advanced RBAC yet)

---

## 0.2 Leads (Manual Only)

**Purpose:** get every opportunity into the pipeline quickly.

### Capabilities
- Create lead manually (one-by-one fields)  
- Lead fields (MVP): name, phone/email, address, service request/job info, notes  
- Lead statuses: **New → Contacted → Estimating → Won → Lost**  
- Convert lead to customer (either explicit conversion or “customer created automatically when lead is created”)

### Non-goals (Phase 0)
- Web forms, lead sources integrations, automated lead capture

---

## 0.3 Customers (Light CRM)

**Purpose:** single record of truth + history.

### Capabilities
- Customer profile (contacts + one or more service locations)  
- Notes  
- Search  
- History timeline: linked leads, estimates, jobs, invoices, payments

---

## 0.4 Estimates (Core + “win area”)

**Purpose:** fast quoting that actually closes.

### Capabilities
- Create estimate from lead/customer  
- Line items (service, qty, unit price), totals, taxes/discounts  
- Attachments (photos) + notes  
- Estimate statuses: **Draft → Sent → Approved / Declined / Expired**  
- Multiple estimates per lead, but enforce only one Approved at a time  
- Send estimate via email/SMS link (one-way only)  
- Customer approval (simple approve flow)

### Non-goals (Phase 0)
- Good/Better/Best, deep pricebook, AI-assisted estimating

---

## 0.5 Deposits (Mode A, gating Job creation)

**Purpose:** enforce commitment before operational work starts.

### Capabilities
- Deposit can be requested only after estimate is Approved  
- Customer pays deposit via payment link  
- Deposit status: **Requested → Paid**  
- Hard gate: no job can be created until deposit is paid  
- Deposit recorded and carried forward as credit later (applied to final invoice)

---

## 0.6 Job Creation & Management (Manual Create Job)

**Purpose:** execute delivery with minimal complexity.

### Capabilities
- Office clicks Create Job after deposit paid (manual control)  
- Job auto-populates from estimate (scope/line items/notes/photos)  
- Job statuses: **Unassigned → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed**  
- Job details: customer/location, scope, internal notes, attachments  
- Assign one or more technicians (start with one if you want to simplify)

---

## 0.7 Scheduling & Dispatch (Simple but dependable)

**Purpose:** office can plan the week without chaos.

### Capabilities
- Calendar (week view)  
- Unassigned jobs queue  
- Assign tech + time window  
- In-app notification to technician on assignment/changes  

### Non-goals (Phase 0)
- Route optimization, skill matching, auto-assignment

---

## 0.8 Technician Experience (Mobile-first essentials)

**Purpose:** tech can complete work without calling the office.

### Capabilities
- Today’s jobs list + job detail view  
- Update job status  
- Add notes + photos  
- Completion confirmation (signature is recommended; can be optional if you want to cut)

### Non-goals (Phase 0)
- Offline mode, time clock, GPS tracking

---

## 0.9 Invoicing

**Purpose:** bill quickly and consistently.

### Capabilities
- Generate invoice from completed job  
- Apply deposit as credit automatically  
- Invoice statuses: **Draft → Sent → Paid** (Partial optional)  
- Send invoice via email/SMS link (one-way)

---

## 0.10 Payments (One-way)

**Purpose:** get paid + track cash.

### Capabilities
- Online payment via invoice link  
- Payment recorded  
- Receipt confirmation

---

## 0.11 Basic Reporting & Analytics (Phase 0)

**Principle:** reports are mostly filtered list views + a few totals, not a complex dashboard.

### A) Pipeline & Sales (Leads/Estimates)
- Leads by status (New/Contacted/Estimating/Won/Lost)  
- Won vs Lost (count + $ based on approved estimates)  
- Estimate funnel: Draft → Sent → Approved/Declined  
- Estimate aging list: “Estimates sent but not approved after X days”

### B) Deposit tracking (critical for your gating model)
- Approved estimates awaiting deposit (action list)  
- Deposits collected (total by date range)  
- Deposit-to-job conversion queue: “Deposit paid, job not created yet” (office action list)

### C) Operations (Jobs/Scheduling)
- Jobs by status (Unassigned/Scheduled/In Progress/Completed)  
- Unassigned jobs queue (action list)  
- Jobs scheduled today / next 7 days  
- Technician workload snapshot (jobs per tech today/this week)

### D) Finance (Invoices/Payments)
- Outstanding invoices list  
- Invoice aging buckets (basic: 0–7 / 8–30 / 31–60 / 60+)  
- Payments received (day/week/month totals)

### E) MVP “Are we switching?” usage signals
**Counts over time:**
- Leads created  
- Estimates sent  
- Deposits paid  
- Jobs completed  
- Invoices paid  

**Simple cycle-time averages (optional but valuable):**
- Approved → Deposit paid (avg days)  
- Completed → Paid (avg days)

### MVP reporting UX (recommended)
- One Reports page with tiles + date range filter  
- Each tile links to a filtered list (no heavy BI)  
- No export required in Phase 0 (consistent with your decision)

---

## Phase 0 explicit exclusions (for scope control)
- Import/export & migrations  
- Two-way SMS / inbox  
- Customer portal  
- Automations/workflow builder  
- Route optimization, GPS, offline mode  
- Inventory, equipment assets  
- Advanced custom report builder / forecasting  
- Integrations marketplace / API  
- AI
