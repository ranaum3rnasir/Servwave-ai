1. **Jobber** - Best for SMBs, simplicity & ease of use

## DETAILED COMPETITOR PROFILES

### 1. JOBBER
**Market Position:** Category leader for SMBs  
**Best For:** Small to mid-sized service businesses (5-50 technicians)  
**Price Range:** $79-329/month  
**Rating:** 4.5/5 (G2 & Capterra)  
**Customer Base:** ~300,000 contractors

#### What Jobber Does Best

✅ **Simplicity & Ease of Use** (🥇 #1 in category)
- Clean, intuitive dashboard
- Minimal learning curve
- Field teams adopt quickly
- Contractor feedback: "It just works"

✅ **Quoting & Estimates** (🥈 #2, behind ServiceTitan)
- Fast estimate creation (3-5 mins)
- Good/Better/Best pricing tiers ready
- Professional quote templates
- Mobile-friendly estimate creation

✅ **Scheduling & Dispatch** (🥈 #2, behind ServiceTitan)
- Calendar view with color-coded technicians
- Drag-and-drop assignment
- Route optimization (basic)
- One-click route generation

✅ **Mobile App** (🥇 #1 in category)
- Field-native experience
- Technician satisfaction high
- Job completion smooth
- GPS tracking included

✅ **Client Portal (Client Hub)** (🥇 #1 in category)
- 24/7 customer access to quotes, appointments, payments
- Reduces inbound calls by 30%
- Professional appearance
- Easy payment collection

✅ **Customer Support** (🥇 #1 in category)
- Unlimited phone/email/chat support
- Response time: 2-5 minutes
- 1-on-1 product coaching included
- Highly rated by users

✅ **Integrations** (Well-rounded, not specialized)
- QuickBooks sync (strong)
- Stripe/Square payments
- Zapier integrations
- Better than HCP, not as deep as ServiceTitan

✅ **Invoicing Automation** (🥈 #2)
- Auto-generate from job completion
- Professional templates
- Batch invoicing
- Payment reminders

✅ **Payment Processing** (🥈 #2)
- Stripe/Square integration
- On-device tap-to-pay
- Instant payouts available
- Multiple payment methods

#### What Jobber Lacks

❌ **Advanced Pricebook** (ServiceTitan beats it)
- Limited customization options
- Can't create complex pricing structures
- Tiered pricing not as flexible

❌ **Reporting & Analytics** (ServiceTitan has 2-3x depth)
- Basic reports only
- Limited customization
- Can't drill down deep
- No marketing ROI tracking
- No profitability by job type
- No custom report builder

❌ **Job Costing** (ServiceTitan dominates)
- Limited labor cost tracking
- Can't track profitability per job
- No margin analysis

❌ **Multi-Location** (ServiceTitan dominates)
- Not built for multi-location ops
- Reporting breaks with multiple locations
- Permission controls not granular enough

❌ **Complex Workflows** (ServiceTitan handles better)
- Can't build custom workflows
- Limited approval processes
- Not for complex, custom operations

❌ **Inventory Management** (Missing entirely)
- No parts/materials tracking
- No van inventory
- Have to use third-party tools
